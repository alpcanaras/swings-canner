How to read this page. Each box is one index. The bold line is the only thing you need: buy dips, sell rallies, or do nothing. Most days it says no trade — that is normal and correct.

Track record is what happened historically after the same setup, measured over ~15 years. A 57% win rate means it was wrong 43% of the time, so no single signal means much; only the average over many trades does.

Exit-if-wrong level is where the idea has failed and you close the trade. Decide it before you enter, not after.

The tables under Numbers behind it are for checking whether a signal still works. A signal whose weight is 0.00 is being ignored on purpose — it has no proven edge.
