How to use this page. These are candidates, not instructions — a slight statistical tilt, nothing more. Expect to be wrong roughly 45% of the time even when everything goes right.

Check the index page first. Don't buy stocks when the market bias is SHORT, don't short them when it's LONG.

Before any trade, decide two things: the price where you admit you're wrong (given for each name), and how much you're risking to that price — keep it around 1% of your account, always the same.

Skip anything flagged for earnings. A gap through your exit level is how small losses turn into large ones.
